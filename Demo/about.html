About Our Gram Panchayat

About Us

Our Gram Panchayat is dedicated to providing transparent, efficient and citizen-friendly governance. We focus on overall village development through digital services, public welfare schemes and community participation.

The Panchayat works continuously to improve education, healthcare, sanitation, drinking water, roads, agriculture and employment opportunities for every citizen.

Our vision is to build a clean, smart, self-reliant and digitally connected village that ensures equal opportunities for all residents.

Our Mission

Deliver transparent administration, quality public services and sustainable rural development.

Our Vision

Create a modern, clean, educated and digitally empowered village for future generations.

Core Values

Transparency, accountability, integrity, participation, innovation and citizen satisfaction.

Objectives

  • Provide better drinking water facilities.
  • Improve sanitation and cleanliness.
  • Support digital governance initiatives.
  • Develop roads and public infrastructure.
  • Promote education and healthcare.
  • Encourage women empowerment and youth development.
  • Implement Government welfare schemes effectively.
