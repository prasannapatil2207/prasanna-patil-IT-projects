About Nimshirgaon

Nimshirgaon is a beautiful village located in Shirol Taluka of Kolhapur District, Maharashtra. The Gram Panchayat is committed to providing quality public services, infrastructure development, cleanliness, water supply and welfare schemes for all villagers.

Our mission is to create a digitally connected, clean, sustainable and prosperous village through active citizen participation and transparent governance.
